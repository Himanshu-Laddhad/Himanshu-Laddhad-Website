About

I'm Himanshu Laddhad, a data scientist with three-plus years in places where the numbers have consequences: operational analytics and forecasting at Bosch and JCB, and most recently the production systems of a B2B integration platform at PartnerLinQ. I finished my MS in Business Analytics and Information Management at Purdue's Daniels School of Business in July 2026, where I also served as a graduate teaching assistant for AI for Business.

The through-line in my work, and the reason this site reads like a memo instead of a gallery, is a preference for models that survive contact with operations. That has meant learning three crafts in sequence: classical forecasting and the model-operations discipline around it, causal inference because "what did we cause" is the question budgets answer to, and LLM systems engineering because agents are becoming how analytics gets delivered. The brief makes that argument section by section, with footnotes.

Before data science I trained as a mechanical engineer at NIT Surat, which is probably why I trust breadth-first traversals of a bill of materials more than most slide decks. Outside of work I write occasionally on Medium, most recently about why one interview coach needed three different LLMs.
